Work / OurNest

Meta Ads · Lead quality

Looking past cost per lead

I designed a way to review paid-social performance with person-level lead and sales feedback, so campaign decisions could account for what happened after a form submission.

  • Meta Ads
  • CRM analysis
  • Real estate

01 · Context

Lead counts alone miss progression

The working workbook combines Meta campaigns, current and historical performance, lead-status records and recommendations. A person may submit more than once or appear in both status sources, so raw lead rows cannot be treated as distinct people.

02 · Diagnosis

Resolve the person before the campaign

I used a normalized phone key to bring a person’s records together. The strongest verified positive stage takes precedence across status fields and follow-up notes. A planned visit remains distinct from a completed visit, while repeat submissions stay available for journey analysis.

03 · Strategy

Join performance and sales feedback

Current media view

Use the current campaign, ad set and ad insight tables for spend and delivery.

Person-level funnel

Count a person once at the highest verified stage while retaining all touches for attribution questions.

Pipeline checks

Inspect stale joins, duplicate lead actions and status conflicts before trusting a derived recommendation.

Decision review

Compare campaign performance with follow-up, visits and booking evidence when available.

04 · Evidence

A documented analysis system

The data guide documents a 22-tab workbook, source hierarchy and reconciliation rules. It also identifies a stale joined view and counts that require repair, so I am keeping public performance figures out of this case until the current Meta and CRM data are reconciled.

05 · Learning

Measure the journey

Cost per lead is an early signal. Decisions improve when campaign reviews also distinguish repeat inquiries, actual contact, completed visits and bookings.
