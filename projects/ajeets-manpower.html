International recruitment · Acquisition

Matching recruitment demand to intent

I worked on digital acquisition and content for an international recruitment audience with different role, destination and candidate needs.

  • Recruitment marketing
  • Campaign content
  • Search intent

01 · Context

Different candidates need different paths

A person researching an overseas role may be exploring a destination, checking a job category or deciding whether to apply. A blended campaign message risks sending those visitors to an irrelevant next step.

02 · Approach

Organize message and destination

I considered role and geography in campaign and content themes, then reviewed the relevance of ads, pages and application paths. The review focused on matching candidate intent to a useful next action.

03 · Learning

Start with the candidate question

Campaign averages are easier to interpret when job category and destination are clear in the structure. That separation helps distinguish useful demand from general browsing.
