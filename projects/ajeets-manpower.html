Work / Ajeets Manpower

B2B manpower demand · Europe & Gulf · Google Ads & SEO

Generating manpower requirement leads across Europe and the Gulf

As Digital Media Strategist at Ajeets Manpower, I worked on Google Ads and SEO to attract businesses in European and Gulf markets seeking recruitment and manpower services.

  • Oct 2022 – Mar 2023
  • Employer lead generation
  • European & Gulf markets
  • Google Ads & SEO

01 · Business context

Reach businesses with workforce requirements

The acquisition objective was to generate enquiries from employers with manpower requirements. The work centred on the commercial demand for recruitment services across European and Gulf markets, with country and service relevance shaping the audience and message.

This required understanding how a business searches for a manpower partner and making the path from search to enquiry relevant to its hiring needs.

02 · Paid acquisition

Match search intent to manpower services

I used Google Ads to capture demand for recruitment and manpower services, with attention to the market, keyword intent, ad messaging and landing-page relevance.

The focus was to connect businesses seeking a recruitment partner with a useful next step. Campaign and content reviews considered whether the search, message and destination addressed the employer’s requirement.

03 · Organic acquisition

Build search visibility across Gulf markets

My SEO work covered on-page, off-page, local and technical optimization. It supported the discoverability of Ajeets’ recruitment services alongside paid search.

The earlier career record documents top-10 keyword rankings across six Gulf countries within six months. This added organic search visibility to the international acquisition work.

04 · International execution

Adapt acquisition to market and service intent

Working across European and Gulf markets strengthened my ability to consider geography and business intent together. A relevant recruitment-service message needs to reflect the market and the workforce requirement behind the enquiry.

The experience built my foundation in international B2B lead generation through a combination of Google Ads, SEO and service-focused content.
