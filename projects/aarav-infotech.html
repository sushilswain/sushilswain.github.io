Agency experience · Paid media · SEO · Social

Agency experience across search, social and paid acquisition

As a Digital Marketing Specialist at Aarav Infotech, I worked on the agency’s own acquisition and managed a portfolio of 17 concurrent client projects across paid media, SEO and social for domestic and international businesses.

  • Jun 2023 – Feb 2025
  • Google · Meta · Microsoft Ads
  • SEO & social media
  • GTM & GA4

01 · Agency scope

Two sides of agency growth

My role combined marketing for Aarav Infotech’s digital services with delivery across its client accounts. The agency’s own acquisition needed to reach businesses seeking services such as SEO. Client work involved different offers, audiences and priorities, requiring an approach suited to each account.

Managing concurrent projects meant keeping execution, measurement and recurring performance reviews organized while adapting campaign and content decisions to each business.

02 · Paid acquisition

Make search demand commercially relevant

For Aarav’s own Search program, I worked on keyword research, search-term review, negative keywords and campaign restructuring. I grouped related intent into focused ad groups and aligned ad messaging with relevant landing pages.

The work also covered bidding, ad scheduling, audience and remarketing decisions, and landing-page recommendations. Google Tag Manager and GA4 supported conversion measurement and the review of meaningful website actions.

Across the agency’s client portfolio, my paid-media responsibilities extended to Google, Meta and Microsoft Ads, with account-specific optimization and performance reviews.

03 · Client projects

SEO, social and paid media across multiple accounts

SEO projects

I led SEO execution, using keyword and search-intent analysis to guide content and optimization priorities for client websites.

Social media projects

My portfolio included social media work alongside SEO and paid acquisition, expanding my experience across client communication and channel execution.

Paid ads projects

I managed campaigns across Google, Meta and Microsoft Ads, adapting targeting, messaging and optimization to each client’s offer and audience.

04 · Measurement & delivery

Connect account activity to useful reporting

I implemented custom GTM tags, triggers and variables for client measurement and used website analytics to support campaign reviews. This technical work helped bring tracking and onsite actions into acquisition decisions.

Recurring reviews across multiple accounts strengthened my ability to identify where a problem required a targeting change, a more relevant landing page, an SEO improvement or a measurement fix.

05 · Experience gained

A foundation in client delivery and diagnosis

Agency work developed my ability to move between businesses, understand their commercial needs and translate those needs into practical marketing work. It built the foundation for my later work in B2B acquisition, funnel analysis and marketing systems.
