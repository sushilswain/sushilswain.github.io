Working approach

How I approach growth problems

01

Understand the business and locate the constraint


I begin with the audience, offer, funnel and sales feedback. I investigate whether the constraint sits in demand capture, campaign delivery, landing-page relevance, tracking or the follow-up process.

02

Connect the evidence


I bring together ad-platform data, website behaviour and CRM information. Where the numbers disagree, I investigate tracking, source mapping and funnel stages before deciding what to change.

03

Make focused improvements


I use the diagnosis to refine targeting, keywords, messaging, landing pages and nurturing. Feedback from sales helps determine whether those changes attract more relevant prospects.

04

Make the process repeatable


I automate recurring reporting and use AI to support analysis. Human review remains part of decisions that affect live campaigns. The goal is a working system that helps the team learn and act consistently.
